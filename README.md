# Growth Audit — SwipeMix

**Live:** https://riddny.github.io/swipemix-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic SwipeMix already has, plus a clickable prototype of the onboarding and paywall rebuilt in SwipeMix's own design. Prepared by Adapty, November 2026.

| # | Idea | Screen | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Give one second chance when they close — "SwipeMix PRO 12 Months (Sale)" at ₪49.90, shown against ₪29.90 a week | Offer on close | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Ask what they want to make first, then open the library on it | What to make → Your library | CR +10–20% |
| 03 | Name their format on the paywall | Paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 04 | Let the trial switch show the yearly plan (₪149.90, ₪2.88 a week) | Paywall | ARPU +10–15% |
| 05 | Show your 4.8 from 6.1K ratings where they decide | Setup, paywall | CR +5–10% |

Run together: potential result up to **+25% CR to purchase** and **+30% ARPU**.

The prototype has English and Russian, light and dark, a 30-second walkthrough, and a QR code to open it on a phone.

**Grounding.** Every price, rating, quote and template name comes from SwipeMix's own App Store listing (US and Israel storefronts), its in-app purchase list and its in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for SwipeMix.
